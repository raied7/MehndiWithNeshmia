About the Artist

A passion painted in henna.

I'm Neshmia — a self-taught mehndi artist who fell in love with the craft watching elders draw delicate vines on Eid mornings. What began as a childhood ritual has grown into a calling: creating heirloom-worthy henna for brides, parties, and life's quiet milestones.

Every booking is personal. I take time to understand your story, your outfit, and the feeling you want to carry on your skin — then design something that's entirely your own.
